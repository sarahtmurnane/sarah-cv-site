About

Dublin-based writer and editor covering music, nightlife, politics, and the things people don't always want to talk about. My work has appeared in Oxygen.ie, Karma Magazine, TCD Misc., Trinity Film Review, and Trinity News. I like to write with honesty, a bit of humour, and without pretending to have all the answers.
